FAQ

Who can use Ssok Uploader?

Ssok Uploader is a desktop app used by its creator to publish their own content to their own TikTok accounts. It does not access or post to anyone else's account.

What TikTok data does the app use?

Only your basic profile (display name and avatar) to show which account you are posting to, and the creator settings TikTok provides for posting (available privacy options and interaction settings). See the Privacy Policy.

Does it post automatically without my confirmation?

No. Every post opens a settings window where you review the content and choose the privacy level before you press Post.

How do I disconnect the app?

In the TikTok app go to Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and websites, and remove Ssok Uploader. You can also delete the local token file on your computer.
